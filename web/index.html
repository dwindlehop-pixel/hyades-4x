Hyades

New game

This is the relay test (T-169). Seats play rounds of hidden orders — commit, reveal, timeout votes, checkpoints — through public Nostr relays, and every relay reply is logged. No galaxy is simulated yet: an order is a card number, and a checkpoint is the hash of the orders every seat played. Nothing here reveals your network address to other players (docs/Hyades_sessions_discovery_and_security.md §4.4).
